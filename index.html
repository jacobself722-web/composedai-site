FAQs

Questions, answered plainly

Does anyone hear my recordings?

No. Your takes stay on your phone, in your private library. Nothing is posted anywhere, and nobody hears a take unless you choose to share it yourself.

How much time does it take?

About a minute a day for the core rep. Lessons and the other practice tools are there when you want more, but one honest minute of talking is the habit.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. Every tool works audio only. Camera is an option for when you want it, never a requirement.

Is the app free?

It's free to download and comes with a free trial of the full program, so you can put in real reps before deciding if it's for you.

What's the deep research mode?

The topic generator has a second gear: it deals you a real concept, like the false positive paradox, gives you ten minutes to look it up, then one minute to explain it out loud. Explaining something is the fastest way to actually understand it.

Is it on Android?

ComposedAI is iPhone first for now. If you want it on Android, email us and tell us, it genuinely affects what we build next.
